    FAQ

    What is a "global reset"?

    OpenAI occasionally resets Codex / ChatGPT usage limits for all paid users at once, refilling everyone's quota. Tibo (@thsottiaux) at OpenAI usually announces these on X.

    How is this data collected?

    Only posts from @thsottiaux that confirm a reset was delivered (e.g. "All propagated", "It's done") are listed, each linked to the source post. Nothing is guessed.

    Can you predict the next reset?

    No. Resets are discretionary. The average gap is shown for fun, not as a forecast.

    Does this change my personal limit window?

    No. Your regular rolling limits still apply; check Codex/ChatGPT settings for your own reset time.
